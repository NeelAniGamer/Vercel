# Pages And Routes

## Vercel URL Routing (`vercel.json`)

- **`cleanUrls: true`**: All `.html` extensions are stripped from public URLs.
- **Rewrites**:
  - `/` &rarr; `/home`
  - `/perceptus` &rarr; `/gesture`
- **Permanent Redirects**:
  - `/index` &rarr; `/home`
  - `/how-it-was-made` &rarr; `/making`
  - `/engine` &rarr; `/solar`
