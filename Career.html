Six Ways People Get Their First Job In These Fields

The routes that actually convert into a full-time offer, and how long each one takes
Six Common Ways People Start

1. Summer Analyst / Associate (8–12 Wks)

Top in Tech (FAANG/Startups), Investment Banking, MBB Consulting, and Corporate Law. Direct pipeline to full-time Pre-Placement Return Offers (PPOs).

2. University Co-op (4–6 Months)

Alternating academic study with full-time industrial engineering work in Automotive, Aerospace, Hardware, and Biotech manufacturing.

3. Statutory Articleship & Pupillage (2–3 Yrs)

Mandatory statutory practical training required by law to earn Chartered Accountant (ICAI / CPA / ACCA) or practicing Court Litigator licensure.

4. Clinical Clerkship & Housemanship (1 Yr)

Compulsory rotatory bedside training for MBBS, BDS, and Nursing graduates across Emergency, Surgery, Pediatrics, and ICU before medical licensure.

5. Elite Research Fellowships

Funded international summer research programs (CERN Openlab, DAAD, Caltech SURF) designed to publish papers for top Ph.D. admissions.

6. Startup Apprenticeships

3–6 month high-velocity roles inside venture-backed startups with direct exposure to founders and production launches.

Turning An Internship Into A Job Offer
  • Agree on what “done” means in week one: confirm the exact feature, report or experiment you will be judged on.
  • Write things down in public: clean pull requests, a short weekly summary, no surprises at review time.
  • Fix something nobody asked you to fix: broken tests, missing docs, a stale data pipeline.
  • Finish with a real demo: show the result, the numbers behind it, and what you would do next.
