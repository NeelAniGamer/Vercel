Tips

01Accuracy first

Mistakes lower your net score. Get to about 95% accuracy before you push for speed.

02Home row

Rest on A S D F and J K L ;. The small bumps on F and J tell you where you are without looking down.

03Short daily practice

Fifteen minutes a day helps more than one long session on weekends.

04Look at the screen

Do not look at your keyboard. Let the next-key hint show which finger to use.

05Shift with the other hand

Use the opposite shift key: right Shift for left-hand letters, left Shift for right-hand letters.

06Relax your hands

Keep wrists loose and just above the desk. Tense fingers type slower.
