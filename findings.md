# Findings: Voiceover Architecture & Project Mapping (No Traffic Hero)

## Project Mapping & Narrative Progression
1. **Solar Engine (`engine.html`, `solar.html`)**:
   - Role: The Inciting Incident.
   - Screen action: Camera pushes into 3D Sun, solar flares erupt, electromagnetic wave strikes Earth's orbit.
   - VO Tone: High-energy urgency, scientific dread, cinematic trailer energy.
2. **Terra3D (`Terra3D/`) + GestureUI (`GestureUI/`, `gesture.html`)**:
   - Role: The Physical Obstacle & Triangulation.
   - Screen action: Mouse cursor dead due to EMP. Hands enter webcam frame: air-pointing rotates the 3D globe, clenching fist locks onto the surviving Indian Ocean relay station coordinates.
   - VO Tone: Physical struggle, comedic tension, frantic coordination.
3. **QR Matrix Studio (`qr-editor.html`)**:
   - Role: The Degraded Visual Cipher.
   - Screen action: Satellite transmission is garbled. Dynamic QR matrix pattern must be adjusted/cleared of noise and scanned to unlock the payload hash.
   - VO Tone: Puzzle-solving race, ticking clock, triumphant breakthrough.
4. **Advanced Typing Instructor - ATI 1.4 (`ati.html`, `ATI 1.4`)**:
   - Role: The Grand Terminal Climax & Final Boss.
   - Screen action: Injecting the emergency kernel restart code. Combo counter rises, WPM must stay above 90, countdown timer ticks under 30 seconds. One typo resets the packet buffer!
   - VO Tone: Peak adrenaline, breathless mechanical keystroke sync, nail-biting finish.

## VO Persona & Directing Notes
- **Persona**: Tech-savvy developer caught in his own chaotic experiment. A mix of *Mark Rober*'s structured excitement and *Michael Reeves*' self-deprecating panic.
- **Pacing**: Starts explosive (first 15 seconds), settles into focused tension during the puzzle phases, and crescendos into hyper-intense keystroke rhythm in the final 90 seconds.
