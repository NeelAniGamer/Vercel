## License Definitions

- **CC0 (Creative Commons Zero):** Public domain. No attribution required. Use freely for any purpose.
- **CC-BY (Creative Commons Attribution):** Free to use for any purpose. Must credit the creator.
- **CC-BY-SA (Attribution-ShareAlike):** Must credit creator and share derivatives under same license.
