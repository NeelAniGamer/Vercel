Data & Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy.

Effective Date: April 2026. We are committed to protecting your privacy across our applications, including ATI, Solar Engine, Gesture Controller, and QR Studio. Read on to learn how we handle your data responsibly.

Architecture

Local-First Processing

Machine-vision gestures, typing calculations, and orbital physics process entirely on your local machine. No sensory telemetry is streamed to our servers.

OAuth Compliance

Limited Use Google Scopes

We request only basic profile identity (name and email) for cross-device sync. Never sensitive or restricted scopes like Google Drive files or Gmail messages.

Data Rights

Guaranteed Deletion

You own your data. Submit an instant purge request through our Grievance Portal to permanently erase your account and database records within 24 hours.

1 Local Processing

We prioritize your privacy by keeping data on your device whenever possible. Most of our software is engineered to run entirely on your local machine without phoning home to external servers. Your projects, calculations, and Perceptus Flow machine-vision gestures are processed locally within your browser or desktop application.

2 Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Our applications may allow you to authenticate using your Google Account. In doing so, we strictly comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We disclose the following regarding your Google user data:

  • Data Accessed: If you choose to sign in with Google, we access your basic profile information (Name and Profile Picture) and your Email Address via the standard Google OAuth scopes (userinfo.profile, userinfo.email). We do NOT request or access any sensitive or restricted scopes like Google Drive files or Gmail.
  • Data Usage: We use this accessed data solely for the purpose of authenticating your identity on our platform and displaying your name and profile picture in your account dashboard. Your email is used as a unique identifier to securely sync your saved items across your devices.
  • Data Storage: Your Name, Email, and Profile Picture URL are securely transmitted and stored in our managed database (Supabase) to maintain your session and sync history. We do not store your Google password or raw OAuth tokens persistently on our backend. All database records are protected by row-level security.
  • Data Sharing: We absolutely do NOT share, sell, transfer, or disclose your Google user data to any third parties, advertisers, or data brokers. Your data is used exclusively to provide the core functionality of the Class Of Learners applications.

3 QR Studio & Dynamic Codes

Static QR codes are created and saved on your device. If you sign in, saved codes can also sync to your account in our database so they follow you across devices.

When someone scans a dynamic code, our redirect page looks up the code and records limited technical information: the scan time, device type, browser, operating system, referring page, and a pseudonymous identifier used to count unique visitors. The owner of the code sees this as analytics for that code. Passcodes are never sent to our servers, and protected destinations are stored in encrypted form. The website a visitor is forwarded to has its own privacy practices. Code owners can delete a code at any time.

4 Data Retention & Deletion

We believe in data minimization. Your feedback and session data are stored securely on Supabase but are not kept indefinitely. If you wish to delete your account or any data associated with it, you can request full deletion via our feedback form, and we will purge your records from our database within twenty-four hours.

5 Analytics, Cookies & Advertising Disclosures

Class Of Learners uses essential cookies solely to retain visual theme preferences and authenticated sessions. In addition, third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve relevant advertisements based on a user's prior visits to our web applications or other websites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to deliver non-intrusive ads. Users may freely opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ads Settings or by consulting AboutAds Choices.

6 Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

While our core simulators and vision modules execute locally on your device, we rely on industry-standard infrastructure partners: Vercel (Edge Hosting), Supabase (Encrypted Data Storage protected by Row-Level Security), and Google AdSense (Publisher Advertising). We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to advertisers or third-party brokers.

7 Contact Us & Grievance Redressal

Developed and maintained by Neel, Ansh, and Aarush as an educational initiative. Under applicable Digital Personal Data Protection standards and Rule 3(2) of the Information Technology Rules, any inquiries concerning data deletion, rectification, or content grievances may be submitted directly to our designated Grievance Officer through the Feedback & Grievance Portal. We acknowledge all submissions within twenty-four hours.
