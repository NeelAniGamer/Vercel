Privacy Policy.

Effective Date: April 2026. We are committed to protecting your privacy across our applications, including ATI, Solar Engine, Gesture Controller, and QR Studio. Read on to learn how we handle your data responsibly.

1
Local Processing

We prioritize your privacy by keeping data on your device whenever possible. Most of our software is engineered to run entirely on your local machine without phoning home to external servers. Your projects, calculations, and gestures are processed locally within your browser or desktop application.

2
Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Our applications may allow you to authenticate using your Google Account. In doing so, we strictly comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We disclose the following regarding your Google user data:

  • Data Accessed: If you choose to sign in with Google, we access your basic profile information (Name and Profile Picture) and your Email Address via the standard Google OAuth scopes (userinfo.profile, userinfo.email). We do NOT request or access any sensitive or restricted scopes like Google Drive files or Gmail.
  • Data Usage: We use this accessed data solely for the purpose of authenticating your identity on our platform and displaying your name and profile picture in your account dashboard. Your email is used as a unique identifier to securely sync your saved items across your devices.
  • Data Storage: Your Name, Email, and Profile Picture URL are securely transmitted and stored in our managed database (Supabase) to maintain your session and sync history. We do not store your Google password or raw OAuth tokens persistently on our backend. All database records are protected by row-level security.
  • Data Sharing: We absolutely do NOT share, sell, transfer, or disclose your Google user data to any third parties, advertisers, or data brokers. Your data is used exclusively to provide the core functionality of the Class Of Learners applications.
3
Data Retention & Deletion

We believe in data minimization. Your feedback and session data are stored securely on Supabase but are not kept indefinitely. If you wish to delete your account or any data associated with it, you can request full deletion via our feedback form, and we will purge your records from our database.

4
Analytics and Cookies

Class of Learners uses minimal cookies solely to maintain your theme preferences and login sessions. We do not use third-party tracking pixels, invasive analytics, or advertisement networks across our site.

5
Third-Party Services

While our core applications run locally, we utilize select third-party services like Vercel (for hosting) and Supabase (for authentication/database). These services comply with strict data protection regulations. We do not integrate ad-networks or sell data to third parties.

6
Contact Us

Developed and maintained by Neel, Ansh and Aarush as a student software initiative. If you have any concerns regarding your privacy or wish to request data deletion, please reach out to us directly through the feedback portal.
