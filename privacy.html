Trust & Transparency

Privacy Policy.

A plain-language overview of how we handle your data across every Class Of Learners application.

📅 Last updated: January 2026
🌏 Applies to: All CoL Applications
🔒 Data handling: Local-first
Section 01

Introduction

We are committed to protecting your privacy across our applications, including ATI, Solar Engine, Gesture Controller, and QR Studio. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used — in plain language, without legal jargon.

💡
Short version: We collect the minimum data required to make the app work. We do not sell it, share it, or use it for advertising.
Section 02

Local Processing

Most of our software is engineered to run entirely on your local machine without phoning home to external servers. Your data stays with you.

Applications like Gesture Controller and Hand Gestures process all webcam and cursor activity locally. Nothing is recorded or transmitted. When you close the app, the session is gone.

Section 03

Google API Services

Our applications may allow you to authenticate using your Google Account. We strictly comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • Data AccessedIf you sign in with Google, we access your basic profile — name, profile picture, and email — via the standard OAuth scopes (userinfo.profile, userinfo.email). We do not request sensitive or restricted scopes.
  • Data UsageThis data is used solely to authenticate your identity and display your name and avatar in your dashboard. Your email is a unique identifier to sync your history across devices.
  • Data StorageYour name, email, and profile picture URL are stored in our managed database (Supabase) to maintain your session. We do not store your Google password or OAuth tokens on our backend.
  • Data SharingWe do not share, sell, transfer, or disclose your Google user data to any third parties, advertisers, or data brokers. Your data is used exclusively to provide core CoL functionality.
Section 04

Contact

This policy is maintained by the Class Of Learners team. If you have questions or concerns, reach out via the feedback page.
