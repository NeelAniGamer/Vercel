What We Believe

Our Mission.

We don't just build projects for grades — we build tools that solve real problems and teach real skills.

🚀

Learn By Building

Every project in our studio exists because we wanted to understand something deeply — orbital mechanics, computer vision, game engines. The best way to learn is to ship.

🌏

Open & Accessible

All of our tools are free to use and accessible from any browser. No downloads, no logins required. Education should have zero barriers.

⚡

Production Quality

We hold ourselves to professional standards — real physics engines, real-time 3D rendering, responsive design, and performance-optimized code.

🤖

Always Evolving

Our studio is a living portfolio. We constantly iterate, redesign, and push the boundaries of what student-built software can look and feel like.

Our Journey

From Idea To Engine.

Early 2024

The Spark

Neel, Ansh, and Aarush began brainstorming ideas for interactive learning tools that went beyond PowerPoint slides and textbook PDFs.

Mid 2024

First Prototypes

The Advanced Typing Instructor and Solar System Engine were born. Rough, buggy, but full of ambition. Sanjana Kasbe joined as project mentor.

Late 2024

Studio Launch

Class of Learners went live with five projects, a unified design language, and a custom-built admin dashboard for remote page management.

2025 — Present

The Next Chapter

Migration to a custom domain, 3D WebGL backgrounds, gesture control prototypes, and an RPG game currently in active development.
