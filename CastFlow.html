What Can CastFlow Do?

Simple tools to put your phone on any display.

Screen Casting

Stream your phone screen to any browser in real time. Perfect for presentations, gaming, or sharing content on bigger screens.

File Sharing

Share any file instantly. Photos, videos, documents — anyone can download directly from their browser without installing anything.

Custom DeX

Turn your phone into a desktop computer. Browser, file manager, calculator — all accessible from any screen.

Remote Control

Control your phone from the browser. Touch gestures, keyboard input, and remote navigation — all supported.

Multi-Viewer

Multiple people can view the same screen simultaneously. Great for collaborative work or sharing with friends.

PDF Support

View PDFs with page navigation, zoom, and rotation. Perfect for documents on the big screen.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Is CastFlow Really Free?

Yes — completely free and open source. No subscriptions, no premium tiers, no hidden fees, and no ads. Ever.

What Do I Need To Run It?

Any Android phone running Android 8.0 or newer, plus a shared Wi-Fi network between your phone and the receiving screen. About 25MB of free space for the install.

Does The TV Or PC Need An App?

No. The receiver only needs a modern web browser. Open the URL CastFlow shows you and the screen, files, and desktop mode appear instantly.

Is My Screen Data Private?

Yes. Streams travel directly over your local Wi-Fi and never touch a cloud server. There are no accounts and nothing is tracked or stored.

Where Can I Report A Bug?

Head to the Feedback Page and send a report with your device model and Android version. Every report is read by the team.
