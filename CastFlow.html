Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

CastFlow is designed to be powerful yet effortless. No bloat, no accounts, no cloud dependency.

📱

Screen Mirroring

High-quality, low-latency mirroring of your Android screen straight to any web browser on your TV, laptop, or tablet.

🖥️

Desktop Mode

Transform your phone into a desktop environment. Open a browser on any device and get a full taskbar, icons, and system tray.

⚡

Instant File Share

Share photos, PDFs, and videos instantly over your local network. Beautiful download pages for every shared file.

🎮

Web Remote Control

Use your PC keyboard and mouse to control your phone. Arrow keys, typing, gestures — all mapped via accessibility services.

📋

Clipboard Sync

Copy text on your phone, paste it in your browser. Or send clipboard contents from any connected device to your phone.

🔒

100% Local

Everything runs on your local Wi-Fi. No data ever leaves your network. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking.

Set up in under 30 seconds.

No drivers. No pairing codes. No companion apps. Just your phone and any device with a browser.

  • Install CastFlow on your Android phone
  • Tap "Start Server" — you get a local URL
  • Open that URL on your TV, laptop, or tablet
  • Start casting, sharing files, or using Desktop Mode
