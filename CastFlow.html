Your Phone. Any Screen.

CastFlow lets you cast your Android screen, share files, and use desktop mode — all from your browser. No cables. No installs on the receiver.

Get CastFlow Free

Why CastFlow?

Powerful features that transform how you use your phone on any display.

Screen Casting

Stream your phone's screen to any browser in real-time. Perfect for presentations, gaming, or sharing content on bigger screens.

File Sharing

Share any file instantly. Photos, videos, documents — anyone can download directly from their browser without installing anything.

Custom DeX

Turn your phone into a desktop computer. Browser, file manager, calculator — all accessible from any screen.

Remote Control

Control your phone from the browser. Touch gestures, keyboard input, and remote navigation — all supported.

Multi-Viewer

Multiple people can view the same screen simultaneously. Great for collaborative work or sharing with friends and family.

PDF Support

View PDFs with page navigation, zoom, and rotation. Perfect for documents, presentations, or reading on the big screen.

How It Works

Three simple steps to cast your screen anywhere.

01

Install CastFlow

Download CastFlow from the Google Play Store on your Android device.

02

Start the Server

Tap to start the web server. CastFlow will show you a URL to open in your browser.

03

Cast Anywhere

Open the URL on any device with a browser. No installation needed on the receiver.
